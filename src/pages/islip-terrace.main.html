About this location

Where Mary's started

This is the one that started it all. Mary's first opened in Islip Terrace back in the '70s, in a different spot in this same lot, and it's been part of the neighborhood ever since. It's a family place: kids at the table, regulars who know the menu by heart, and pizza and pasta made the way Islip Terrace has always liked them.

Weeknights fill up for our specials: Chicken Night Monday through Wednesday and Seafood Night on Thursday, three courses for one price. Planning something bigger? The private room is yours for birthdays, showers and family celebrations, and we cater for offices and parties too.
