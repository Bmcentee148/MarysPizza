About this location

Pizza, pasta and a full bar on the East End

Our Speonk spot lives in the old Olde Speonk Inn on Montauk Highway, rebuilt from the ground up and turned back into the kind of place where everybody's welcome. Hand-tossed pies come out of the brick ovens, the pasta and chicken dishes come out of the kitchen, and the bar pours cocktails, beer and wine.

It's the only Mary's with a full bar, so come in for a slice and stay for a drink. Planning something bigger? We cater for offices and parties, and the private room is yours for birthdays, showers and team dinners.
