Our Story

A family recipe that turned into a neighborhood spot

Mary's started the way the best places do — one family, a stack of recipes handed down for generations, and a stubborn belief that fresh beats fast every time. Years later, the sauce still simmers all morning, the dough is still made by hand, and half the town knows their order by heart.

Come in for a slice, stay for a Sunday-style dinner, or let us cater your next big thing. Either way, you're eating like family here.
